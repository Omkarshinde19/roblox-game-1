# Parked idea: Revenge sequel (Game 2)

Status: **parked** (user, 2026-11-08). We're building the heist first. This
is saved for later as a possible sequel set in the same world.

## Pitch (the user's)
- You play **The Admin's son**.
- Scam's robots have taken over the world. Your dad owned the last piece
  of RAM, so the robots pulled the chip out of his **pacemaker**, and he
  died. A very emotional opening scene.
- You slowly grow stronger, learn to shoot, and fight Scam's robots.
- Finale: you take down Scam in **his own factory** and give the world an
  AI-free future.

## Notes from discussion
- It works as a sequel to Chip Heist: The Admin (the heist's antagonist)
  becomes the dad, so the two games share one story.
- **Content risk:** killing a parody of a real, living person is much
  riskier under Roblox rules than the heist's satire. Suggested fix: by
  the finale Scam has uploaded himself into the factory's AI, and the
  player destroys the machine, not the man.
- Scope is much bigger than the heist: a shooter, a progression system,
  enemy robot AI, a factory level and a boss fight.
