# Chip Heist — Basic Script (Draft v0.1)

A rough first pass for you to rewrite by hand. It follows `story-bible.md`.
Things still undecided are marked **[TBD]**.

**Format**
- `CHARACTER:` line = voiced line in the cheerful AI-assistant voice
- `(ADMIN — GLITCHED)` = The Admin's distorted voice after he wakes
- `[CAM]` = camera / scene cut
- `[SFX]` = sound
- `[PLAY]` = gameplay beat

**Who's who**
- **SCAM**: any player. Role lines: **PARANOID** (Lookout), **LOWERCASE**
  (Hacker), **HYPE** (Carrier), **PODCAST** (Distraction). With fewer than
  4 players, the missing role's lines are skipped or given to another Scam.
- **ADMIN**: The Admin. **MOM**: The Admin's mom. **CAT**: the cat.
- **ROBOTAXI**: the crew's car. **ANCHOR**: TV news anchor. **HOST**: podcast host.

## 01 — The Shortage (cutscene)

[CAM] Black. Caption: **"APPALACHIA. YEAR 2221."**

[CAM] A hologram news feed flickers on in mid-air. A ticker scrolls under
it: "RAM NOW PRICED PER GRAM".

ANCHOR: Good evening, and welcome to the year 2221! RAM prices are up
again, and they're up for a great reason. Data centers need it more than
you do! A stick of memory now costs more than rent. Experts suggest
renting your RAM by the hour.

[CAM] The feed swipes to a hologram podcast: two glowing chairs floating
in the dark. A parody SCAM sits across from the HOST. Caption under him:
"Scam Altman. CEO of ClosedAI. Still."

HOST: Two hundred years as CEO. How are you still here?

SCAM: I uploaded myself. A few times. It scales really well.

HOST: And people are mad they can't afford RAM anymore.

SCAM: Honestly? Everyone keeping RAM at home is kind of a terrible use
of it. Your PC sleeps most of the day. A data center never sleeps.

HOST: So... what do you do about it?

[CAM] Slow push-in on SCAM's hologram face. It flickers. A GPU is
reflected in his eyes.

SCAM: Reallocate.

[SFX] Bass drop. [CAM] Smash cut to black.

## 02 — ClosedAI Briefing (cutscene + role pick)

[CAM] A glass meeting room at ClosedAI HQ, 2221. The whiteboard reads
"AGI: need 3 more RAM". A hologram projector hums.

[CAM] A hologram of SCAM hosts the meeting. Around the table sit the
players: his uploaded copies, all identical.

SCAM: Team. Great to see me. All of me. Let's talk about compute.

[CAM] Slide 1 is a lone cabin on a foggy mountainside, one warm light
in the dark forest.

SCAM: Appalachia. Unknown territory. One cabin on a mountain, three
kilometres from our forest data center. His lights flicker every night.
That's us, draining the grid. Great progress.

[CAM] Slide 2 is a Dizcord profile: "The Admin — Online 24/7 — Server
Moderator since forever".

SCAM: Meet The Admin. He moderates a server with forty members. He has
never left his chair. And in his basement he has the best GPUs left in
Appalachia.

[CAM] Slide 3 is the cabin map: Ground, Basement, Attic.

SCAM: Ground floor, then the basement, then the attic. Somewhere up
there is the legendary Golden DIMM. We take everything, for humanity.

[PLAY] Role pick. Each player clicks a seat. The camera cuts to that
Scam for their intro line.

PARANOID: Okay, okay. I'm on lookout. Everyone pls chill. Nobody touch anything loud.

LOWERCASE: hacker here. doorbell cams are easy. so is ethics

HYPE: CARRIER! I can feel the AGI already! How many GPUs can I hold? MORE!

PODCAST: I'll handle distraction. And let me explain why, because it's actually really interesting.

SCAM: Love the energy. It's mine. Robotaxi's outside.

## 04 — Stage 1: Ground Floor (gameplay)

**Goal:** get in, loot the ground floor, and find the basement door.

[PLAY] Entry points: front door (doorbell cam, which the Hacker loops),
back slider, doggy door (one Scam crawls in and opens the slider),
garage keypad.

LOWERCASE (looping cam): cam loop active. we are now invisible. probably

[PLAY] MOM patrols the kitchen and living room. The CAT wanders.

MOM (humming, peppy): What a wonderful evening for doing laundry nobody asked me to do!

CAT (peppy AI voice): Meow! I'm happy to knock this over for you!
[SFX] A vase smashes.

PARANOID: The cat. The cat is a security risk. Pls chill, cat.

[PLAY] Loot: old towers in the garage (CPUs), a console in the living
room, a "spare RAM" box.

HYPE (picking up loot): Ooh, DDR4! It's like finding a fossil! More!

[PLAY] **Dinner timer.** Every few minutes:

MOM (shouting down the stairs): Sweetie! DINNER!

ADMIN (offscreen, peppy, muffled): Coming, Mom! Great question! Five more minutes!

[PLAY] If Mom spots a Scam:

MOM: Oh! Are you one of his internet friends? He doesn't have any of those.

[PLAY] When Mom is distracted, the Distraction can use a fake Dizcord ping or the TV.

PODCAST: What's interesting about distractions is that people respond to novelty. I'll just turn the TV on.

**Exit:** the basement door. A sign on it reads "DO NOT ENTER. MOD ONLY".

[CAM] First-person from the lead Scam, creeping down the stairs. Every
step creaks. The light turns RGB: purple, then blue, then pink.

[SFX] Heavy, slow snoring. A distant Dizcord ping.

[CAM] Reveal: a huge gaming chair with three monitors. THE ADMIN is
asleep in it, a headset on and a bowl of chips on his chest. His rig
glows behind him like a shrine.

ADMIN (asleep, peppy): Mmm... thank you for being part of the community... you are now muted...

HYPE (whispering): Look at it. Two GPUs. Liquid cooled. I think I'm going to cry.

PARANOID (whispering): Nobody. Breathe.

## 06 — Stage 2: Basement (gameplay)

**Goal:** loot The Admin's rig without waking him.

[PLAY] The Admin cycles: sleeping → stirring → snack run upstairs →
back to his chair.

ADMIN (stirring, peppy): Snack o'clock! I love snacks. Snacks love me.

[PLAY] **2-person GPU carry.** Two Scams must stay close together.

HYPE: Lift with your legs! Lift with your compute!

[PLAY] **Breaker box.** The Lookout flips it for a brownout. The Admin's
stream cuts out.

PARANOID: Breaker's flipped. If he asks, it was the data center. Which, honestly, is believable.

ADMIN (half-awake, peppy): Huh? Power's out again? Stupid data center...

[PLAY] **Hacker on The Admin's PC:** wipe his ban list to buy time.

LOWERCASE: deleting his scheduled bans. 4,000 of them. this guy banned someone for saying hi

[PLAY] Suspicion lines (the RGB headset turns yellow):

ADMIN (peppy, suspicious): Hello? Is someone in my server room? That's a rule violation! A friendly one!

[PLAY] When the main loot is grabbed, cut to 07.

## 07 — He Wakes (cutscene)

[CAM] Tight shot of The Admin's face. His eyes snap open. **Jump scare.**

[SFX] Static, then the peppy voice glitches.

ADMIN: Hello! Welcome! You are... you are... you are...

ADMIN (GLITCHED): ...BANNED.

[CAM] His headset glows red. He stands up. The chair creaks loudly.

[CAM] Wide shot: the basement stairs are blocked by his bulk. The only
way out is the attic ladder.

PARANOID: UP. GO UP. Everyone go up!

PODCAST: Running is actually a really fascinating survival mechanism—

HYPE: RUN, PODCAST!

## 08 — Stage 3: Attic (gameplay chase)

**Goal:** grab the Golden DIMM and reach the roof window.

[PLAY] The Admin climbs after the crew. He's slow on stairs but fast in
straight lines. Two Scams hold the ladder while the others climb.

ADMIN (GLITCHED): You have been warned. You have been warned. You have been WARNED.

[PLAY] The Golden DIMM shrine. A spotlight, and a choir sting.

[SFX] Choir: "aaaaah".

HYPE: The Golden DIMM. One terabyte. I'm feeling the AGI so hard right now.

[PLAY] **Ban Hammer.** A caught Scam goes to `#timeout` (a jail cell),
and a teammate "appeals" to revive them.

ADMIN (GLITCHED, slamming the hammer): Muted. Kicked. BANNED.

SCAM (in timeout, peppy): I've been banned! I'd love to appeal!

[PLAY] If all Scams are in timeout → **Fail ending (F)**.

[PLAY] Reach the roof window → cut to 09.
