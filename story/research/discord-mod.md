# Research: the "Discord Mod" meme (guard/boss NPC)

> For the co-op heist parody. Players are a Sam Altman parody stealing RAM,
> GPUs and chips "for the data center"; the house owner is the meme Discord
> mod. Everything here is meant to stay PG and Roblox-safe.
> Researched 2026-11-04.

---

## 1. The meme

### Origins (short version)
| When | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2007–2008 | **"Mods are asleep"**: the old 4chan/forum line meaning moderators are inactive, so post whatever you want. | [KYM: Mods Are Asleep](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/mods-are-asleep) |
| 2010s | **"He does it for free"**: 4chan's jab at volunteer mods ("janitors") who supposedly abuse their power. | [KYM editorial: Moderators vs Users](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/moderators-vs-users-a-conflict-as-old-as-the-internet) |
| Oct 2013 | **Tips Fedora / neckbeard** look: started as a Reddit photo, later used as shorthand for a stereotype. | [KYM: Tips Fedora](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tips-fedora) |
| 2015–2021 | **"Touch grass"** spreads and peaks on Twitter in 2021. | [KYM: Touch Grass](https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/memes/touch-grass) |
| Aug 2019 → peak Nov 2020 | **Discord Mod meme** spreads as Discord grows: the self-important mod. | [Daily Dot: Discord mod meme](https://www.dailydot.com/memes/discord-mod-meme) |
| Jun 2020 | **"Discord mods, please keep memes out of #general"**: a meme mocking mods' rule reminders. | [KYM entry](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/discord-mods-please-keep-memes-out-of-general) |

- Daily Dot sums up the archetype as "vainglorious, prideful, and self-aggrandizing." ([Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/memes/discord-mod-meme))
- The best-known image is a *Gumball*-style figure: headset, glasses, stubble, and overdramatic movements. TikTok skits (2020) act the mod out as someone who snaps over tiny things.
- Discord mod jokes continue a much older habit of roasting moderators (forum mods, 4chan janitors, Reddit mods).
- **Avoid:** some versions of the meme paint mods as creepy or predatory. That is **off-limits** for us. Keep only the parts about power trips, laziness, snacks and the gamer cave.

### Classic tropes (the ones we can use)
| Trope | How it reads in-game |
|---|---|
| **"Doing it for free"** | He is fiercely proud of an unpaid job and treats "moderator" like a military rank. |
| **Ban-hammer power trip** | A giant glowing hammer, and bans handed out over nothing. |
| **Temp mutes / timeouts** | "Ladies, settle down, I need to temp mute someone" energy ([Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/memes/discord-mod-meme)). |
| **Self-awarded roles and medals** | Shoulder badges like "Head Mod", "Senior Mod", "Mod of the Month (self-nominated)". |
| **Rule recitals** | "Rule 7." "Read #rules." "This is your final warning" (said for the 40th time). |
| **"Keep it in #general" / wrong channel** | Room signs work like channels: `#kitchen`, `#bedroom`, `#off-topic` (the bathroom). |
| **@everyone pings** | His panic alarm. Every phone and speaker in the house goes DING. |
| **"Mods are asleep"** | The main stealth window: while he's asleep, the players are free to move. |
| **Gamer cave** | RGB everything, a cracked gaming chair, three monitors, LED strips, a blackout curtain. |
| **Snacks** | Energy-drink cans (use an off-brand name like "Mountain Doom"), cheese-puff dust, pizza boxes, a cup-noodle tower. |
| **Anime posters / figure shelf** | Use generic chibi art. No real IP. |
| **Body pillow** | Keep it **G-rated**: a long cushion printed with his own Discord pfp or a cartoon frog. That's the joke. |
| **Fedora** | Optional costume piece that he tips when "greeting" intruders. Skip "m'lady" because of its baggage; see section 4. |
| **"Touch grass"** | He says it to others but has never done it himself. Possible ending gag. |

## 2. Character design: the Mod NPC

**Working name:** *ModeratorMax* / "Head Mod Dave" / **"xX_BanLord_Xx"** (gamertag shown over his head).

### Look
- A round, soft cartoon Roblox body (Rthro-ish), drawn the same as any chunky cartoon character. No gross-out close-ups.
- Oversized **RGB gaming headset** that pulses. Its color shows his state: blue = idle, yellow = suspicious, red = alert.
- Glasses that reflect monitor glow, and a fuzzy stubble beard.
- A fedora (optional cosmetic, could be a cosmetic drop).

### Outfit
- A faded hoodie printed "STAFF", or a T-shirt reading **"I DO IT FOR FREE"**.
- Pajama pants and fuzzy slippers that squeak and work as a sound cue when he walks.
- A lanyard with a laminated "MODERATOR" badge (self-made, wonky lettering).
- A sash of self-awarded medals that jingle when he runs.

### Props and set dressing
- **The Ban Hammer**: a huge, comically soft-looking glowing hammer and his catch tool.
- The **gaming chair throne**: it creaks, and leaning back in it is a sleep cue.
- Triple monitors running a fake chat client ("Dizcord") with a scrolling chat. Reading it gives hints about where the loot is.
- A mini-fridge plus the main fridge (patrol points), a pizza-box tower, an energy-can pyramid.
- A **ring light** that switches on when he alerts (doubles as a spotlight).
- A wall poster reading "RULES" with 147 numbered rules.
- Loot nests: RAM sticks in a cereal bowl, a GPU used as a cup holder, chips in a snack bag.

### Voice
- Nasal, overly formal, talks like a policy document. Typing sounds every time he speaks.
- Refers to himself in the third person as "the staff team".
- Uses chat slang out loud ("lol", "brb", "ratio", "L + banned").

## 3. AI / stealth behaviour ideas

### State machine (suggested)
`Sleeping → Groggy → Idle (eating/gaming) → Suspicious → Alerted/Chasing → Catch → back to Idle/Groggy`
- The headset color and a gamertag tag over his head show his state clearly for teen players.
- **Noise meter:** sprinting, knocking things over, and stepping on empty cans add noise. Crouching is quiet.

### Mechanics grounded in the meme
| Idea | How it plays |
|---|---|
| **"Mods are asleep" window** | He sleeps in the chair in cycles. A banner pops up: "MODS ARE ASLEEP" (go-time). |
| **Discord notification pings** | Phones and laptops around the house randomly go *DING*. Each ping has a chance to wake him. Players can **silence devices** (an interact task) or **trigger** one somewhere else as a distraction. |
| **@everyone alarm** | Triggering certain traps (a webcam, a "raid detector" doorbell) sets off a house-wide @everyone ping that puts him straight into Alert. |
| **Food delivery distraction** | A player can order pizza from a house tablet. The doorbell rings and he waddles to the door for about 20 s, saying "Is this the extra-cheese one?" |
| **Fridge patrol** | His patrol route runs chair → fridge → microwave → bathroom ("#off-topic") → chair. Patrols get faster as the night goes on. |
| **Snack-trail lure** | Drop cheese puffs to lead him. He stops and eats each one ("Ooh, floor snack."). |
| **Ban Hammer catch** | If he reaches a player: a bonk with a cartoon "BANNED" stamp, then the player goes to the **Ban Jail**. |
| **Ban Jail / Timeout channel** | A cage room styled as a `#timeout` channel. Teammates free the player by "submitting an appeal": a quick-time or button puzzle at a terminal. |
| **Mute** | His ranged ability is a "MUTE" beam. A hit player can't emote, use text chat, or use the ping wheel for 15 s. Only team comms are affected. Voice can't be disabled for real; show it as a muted-icon debuff. |
| **Slowmode** | Another ability: the hit player moves at half speed with a "Slowmode: 10s" HUD. |
| **Rule recital stun** | When he spots someone at a distance he stops to read a rule aloud (about 2 s), which gives the player a beat to escape. It's a fair-play telegraph. |
| **Wrong-channel detection** | Rooms are channels. Getting spotted in `#bedroom` (his vault) triggers instant Alert. `#kitchen` only triggers Suspicious. |
| **Mod logs camera** | His monitor shows "audit logs" of recent noises with room names. Players can sneak in and **clear the logs** to lower his suspicion. |
| **Self-nominated role upgrades** | Difficulty scaling: each loot item stolen gives him a new medal and role ("Senior Mod"), plus a speed or vision buff. |
| **Boss phase (finale)** | Once most of the loot is gone he goes "Admin Mode": the ring light on, sunglasses, the hammer glowing, and the music switching to dubstep. |
| **Auto-mod bot** | A Roomba "AutoMod" drone patrols while he sleeps. This is a nod to KYM noting that bots replaced sleeping mods ([KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/mods-are-asleep)). |
| **Co-op hooks** | One player distracts (orders pizza, sends a ping) while others grab loot. Two-person lifts for the GPU tower. Downed players need an "appeal". |

Server-side note for implementation: the NPC state, catches, mute and jail must all be server-authoritative. The client only plays VFX and audio.

## 4. Tone note: affectionate roast, not body-shaming

**The joke is his priorities, not his body.** He'd rather guard an online server than live a life, and that is what's funny.

Do:
- **Make laziness about his choices**: he schedules naps "between mod shifts" and treats snacks as "staff rations". The humor comes from the self-importance.
- **Give him competence**: when he's alert he's genuinely fast and scary, like a boss. A guard who is a real threat earns respect, which makes him lovable rather than pathetic.
- **Give him dignity beats**: he's polite to the pizza driver, he has a plant he talks to, and he mods a server about cats. Teens will root for him a little.
- **Keep it self-aware**: he's in on the meme ("Yes, I do it for free. Because I love this community.").
- **Make catches slapstick** (bonk, stamp, cartoon jail), never humiliation.
- **Keep the visual style equal**: the Altman parody players should be just as exaggerated (hoodie, oversized tablet, "for the data center" catchphrase), so both sides get roasted.
- **Roast the tech**: RAM in a cereal bowl, "my GPU is a cup holder". That keeps the punchline on objects and habits.

Avoid:
- Jokes about weight, smell, hygiene, "gross" close-ups, or fat-suit animations (no belly-jiggle gags, no "he can't fit" jokes).
- Predatory or creepy versions of the meme (Daily Dot notes harsher versions exist), anything romantic or sexual about body pillows or "the server's kitten", and "m'lady" with its misogyny baggage ([KYM Tips Fedora](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tips-fedora)).
- **Modeling the NPC on any real person.** The fedora meme came from a real person's photo, so keep the design original.
- Slurs, and the old "mods are gay" chant from Reddit meme subs.
- Using real brand logos (Discord, Mountain Dew, Doritos). Use parody names ("Dizcord", "Mountain Doom", "Cheez Dust"), which also keeps us clear of IP and Roblox moderation issues.
- **A real-person check for the Altman parody**: keep it clearly cartoonish, like a hoodie-wearing "Tech CEO" archetype with a name like "Sam Altmode". No real likeness or defamatory claims. Check this against Roblox's community standards on real people.
