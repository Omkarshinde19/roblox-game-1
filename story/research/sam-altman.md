## 1. The inspiring quote: verified, but the viral version stretches it

**Verdict:** It's real and recent. The viral version ("your chips should go to data centers") goes a bit further than what he actually said. He never said to take anyone's RAM. He argued that RAM sitting in personal PCs is a **poor allocation** of scarce memory compared with RAM working full-time in the cloud. For satire, the real line is funnier anyway: he called it *"quite terrible"* for people to have RAM.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | *Cheeky Pint* podcast (YouTube), hosted by Stripe co-founder **John Collison** |
| **When** | The clip went viral in late October 2026. Futurism covered it 2026-10-30; follow-ups ran through 2026-11-02 |
| **Topic** | Making compute cheaper, and the chip/memory supply crunch |

**What he said (short quotes, via Futurism):**
- "A lot of people want local inference."
- "the world is going to be limited in its silicon capacity"
- "quite terrible to have everybody have a bunch of the world's limited supply" (of DRAM)
- "You want maximum utilization in the cloud"
- His argument: a home PC's RAM sits idle most of the time (one outlet cites about 5% utilization), so the world gets more out of that memory in data centers.
- Caption on the viral clip: "Local inference is a terrible allocation of scarce resources."
- In the same chat he said he wants a "compute glut," meaning cheap chips for everyone, and would be "very sad" if compute costs stopped falling. (MindStudio)

**Reaction:**
- PC gamers, especially r/pcmasterrace, were furious. Many read it as "people shouldn't own computers."
- **Conflict-of-interest angle:** OpenAI has reportedly reserved about 40% of global DRAM supply, so the efficiency argument happens to suit the biggest buyer.
- Townhall op-ed headline: "Your RAM Belongs in AI Data Centers... The Free Market Lets You Disagree."
- The "rent, don't own" critique ties this to his earlier pitch of AI sold "on a meter" like electricity (March 2026).
- I found no official OpenAI walk-back.

  - Coverage has also cited a 128GB DDR5 kit at about $3,399.
- **GPUs:** AMD and Nvidia card prices rose because GDDR memory competes with AI demand for the same fab capacity ([Tom's Hardware on AMD +10%](https://tomshardware.com/pc-components/gpus/amd-to-raise-graphics-card-prices-by-at-least-10-percent-in-2026-price-surge-attributed-to-ongoing-ai-related-dram-supply-crisis)). ## 2. Public persona (caricature reference)

Everything here is public and widely known. Exaggerate it, don't copy it.

**Look**
- Slim, slight build, boyish face. He reads as younger and smaller than people expect.
- Short, slightly tousled brown hair, often with a small swoop. Big, intense eyes.
- Wardrobe: plain crewneck sweaters and tees in grey, navy or green. Startup-founder vests, jeans, sneakers. Basically never a tie.
- Posture: leans forward, hands clasped or gesturing small. Very calm, earnest, slightly wide-eyed.

**Speech and mannerisms**
- Soft-spoken and measured. Long pauses, then something huge said casually ("this will be the most important technology ever").
- Verbal tics: "super," "the world," "I think," "it's, like...", "we're gonna."
- Posts in all-lowercase on X, short and cryptic, sometimes a single word or a teaser before a launch (e.g., the one-word "her" post before the 2024 voice demo). The lowercase style is widely observed; I didn't find a formal source, but it's common knowledge.
- Swings between hype and walk-back: "confident we know how to build AGI" (Jan 2025), then tells fans to "chill" and "cut your expectations 100x" ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/301892/openai-altman-slams-brakes-o3-hype-agi)).

**Recurring themes and catchphrases**
- **Compute, compute, compute.** Scaling, more GPUs, more power. He has said OpenAI needs a significant fraction of Earth's power ([Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/software-apps/sam-altman-openai-needs-significant-fraction-of-earth-power)).

- **"Feel the AGI."** OpenAI's internal chant, which Altman used about GPT-4.5 in Feb 2025 ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/08/25/tech-agi-hype-vibe-shift-superpowered-ai/)).
- **"Intelligence too cheap to meter,"** and later AI sold "on a meter" like electricity.
- **Stargate:** the giant $500B data-center project.
- **Not verified in this pass, from memory (re-check before quoting):**
  - "we are out of GPUs" (GPT-4.5 launch, Feb 2025)
  - users saying "please" and "thank you" costs "tens of millions of dollars" (Apr 2025)
  - reports he sought up to "$7 trillion" for chip fabs (Feb 2024)
  - "The Intelligence Age" and "The Gentle Singularity" blog essays
  - Worldcoin/World iris-scanning "Orb"
  - fired and rehired in five days (Nov 2023)

## 3. Comedic angles: gags and quips for the parody Sam

1. **Picks up a RAM stick:** "this is just... sitting here. 95% idle. that's honestly kind of terrible."
2. **Stealth line while sneaking:** "shh. i'm not stealing. i'm *reallocating*."
3. **Loot screen label:** "Compute Liberated: 32GB." The tally bar is the "Global Utilization Meter."
4. **Grabs the GPU:** "feel the AGI." The squad emote is a whispered group chant: *feel... the... AGI...*
5. **Stealing too much at once overheats the loot bag:** "our backpacks are melting. pls chill." It forces a cooldown.
6. **Mod catches a player:** "this is a free trial of not owning things. you'll love it."
7. **Running joke where every room is "just one more data center":** "the closet? stargate 2."
8. **Leaves an IOU on the empty PC case:** "intelligence too cheap to meter. (bill to follow)."
9. **Hype then walk-back:** before the vault, "this will change everything." After, "ok cut your expectations 100x."
10. **The Discord mod's rig RGB starts blinking:** "is it... sentient? no. but it could be. in a few thousand days."
11. **Fail state is the "board vote":** if the team gets caught, you're fired, then a 5-second timer rehires you ("i love this team so much").
12. **Cryptic lowercase chat bubbles as squad pings:** "here." / "ram." / "her." (the last one for spotting the mod).
13. **Final loot target is the mod's prized RTX card:** "the world has a shared interest in this card. specifically me."
14. **The mod's counter-move:** banning Sam from the server mid-heist ("you have been timed out for 10 minutes"), which the parody Sam answers with "local moderation is a terrible allocation of scarce resources."
15. **Upgrade shop "please & thank you":** saying thanks to an NPC costs the team "tens of millions" of in-game compute.

## 4. Legal and platform caution

**What Roblox's Community Standards actually say** (https://about.roblox.com/community-standards):

| Rule | Short text | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| Impersonation (Harassment and Discrimination) | "Impersonating individuals... in ways that may damage their reputation" | A clearly comedic, obviously-fake parody is lower risk. Never present it as the real person or put fake "real" statements in his mouth as fact. |
| Misleading content | "Deceptive, sensational... or otherwise misleading content or metadata" | Don't use his real name or the OpenAI/ChatGPT logo in the title, thumbnail or keywords to farm discovery. |
| Real people's media | restricts media of "non-public figures" | He's a public figure, but Roblox separately disallows uploading real human faces/photos as assets (per community guides; secondary source). Keep the look stylized. |
| Political figures | bans "Current or recent real-world government officials" | Altman isn't an official, so this doesn't apply. Keep politicians (Trump, Stargate press events, etc.) out entirely. |
| Bullying | bans "Singling out a user or group for ridicule" | Don't make the Discord mod an obvious caricature of a real streamer or mod. Keep the mod fictional. |
| IP | "respect the intellectual property rights of others" | No OpenAI, ChatGPT, Discord, Nvidia, Samsung or SK Hynix logos, and no real product names on textures. Parody those too (e.g., "DisCord" → "Diss Chord", "NVIDEO", "OpenEye"). |

- **Satire carve-out (secondary source):** A 2021 Roblox ToS update reportedly exempted "satire, fan art, or tributes" that aren't misleading from the impersonation/misrepresentation ban ([ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/what-do-the-new-terms-of-service-tos-mean-for-roblox-2021/)). I couldn't fetch the current ToS text (403). Verify it at https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004647846-Roblox-Terms-of-Use before launch.
- **Right of publicity / US law (general, not legal advice):** Parody and commentary about a public figure is generally protected expression. The risk goes up when you use the real name or likeness commercially (paid game passes, merch) or imply endorsement.

**Recommendation**
1. **Use a parody name, not "Sam Altman."** Good options:
   - **"Sam Altmann"**: subtle, reads as a parody to everyone who gets it, and safe.
   - **"Scam Altman"**: funniest, but it's a defamation-flavored insult and could be reported as harassment. I'd avoid it as the character name and maybe keep it as a one-off joke at most.
   - Also fine: "Sam Alt-Tab," "Ctrl-Alt-Man," "Sam ALTman."
2. **Stylized, non-photoreal avatar.** Blocky Roblox proportions plus signature cues: grey crewneck, swoopy hair, huge earnest eyes. No real face textures or photos.
3. **Fictional company:** "OpenEye" / "ClosedAI" / "Stargate" → "StarGRAB."
4. **Clear parody signals:** a game description line like "A parody. Not affiliated with any real person or company." Keep his real name and real logos out of the title, thumbnail and tags.
5. **Punch at the public stance (compute hunger, the RAM comment), not the person's private life, family or sexuality.** Keep it all-ages, since Roblox maturity labels apply.
6. **Don't quote him verbatim as dialogue** in a way that could be mistaken for real statements. Paraphrased riffs are better satire anyway.
